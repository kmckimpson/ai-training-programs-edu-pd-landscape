\# Methodology: Expanded Scholar Discovery Sample



\## Dataset Size

119 papers



\## Purpose



A second Google Scholar discovery scrape was conducted to reduce potential query bias present in the initial dataset and broaden coverage of the AI-in-education research landscape.



The expanded scrape used a wider set of search terms to capture a broader range of discussions surrounding artificial intelligence in teaching and learning.



\## Data Collection



Additional Google Scholar discovery searches were conducted using expanded keyword combinations including:



\- "artificial intelligence education"

\- "generative AI learning"

\- "AI teaching tools"

\- "AI in teaching"

\- "AI classroom use"

\- "large language models education"

\- "ChatGPT education"



Search results were exported into a structured dataset containing:



\- Title  

\- Abstract snippet  

\- Authors  

\- Publication year  

\- Source repository  

\- Article URL  



Duplicate records and inaccessible articles were removed during dataset cleaning.



\## Classification



The same classification taxonomy used in the initial sample was applied to the expanded dataset in order to maintain comparability across the two samples.



Each paper was manually assigned to a single primary category based on the dominant theme visible in the title and abstract.



Classification categories included:



\- Academic Integrity \& Governance  

\- AI Integration \& Educational Transformation  

\- Assessment \& Learning Analytics  

\- Teacher Workflow Support  

\- Interactive \& Experiential Learning  

\- Student Support \& Accessibility  

\- Instructional Personalization  

\- Digital Learning Infrastructure  

\- Institutional Operations
