# Mapping AI-in-Education Research Topics (2023–2026)

This repository contains an exploratory corpus analysis examining how current research literature discusses Artificial Intelligence in Education (AIED).

The project uses Google Scholar discovery searches to construct two datasets of recent research papers (2023–2026). Each paper was manually classified according to its primary research topic.

## Research Question

What topics dominate current research discourse on AI in education?

## Key Finding

Across two independent samples, **Academic Integrity and Governance** emerges as the largest topic category in the literature.

* Initial sample: 20 of 40 papers (50%)
* Expanded sample: 45 of 119 papers (\~37%)

In both datasets, **AI Integration \& Educational Transformation** is the second largest topic cluster.

## Limitations



This dataset represents an exploratory Google Scholar discovery corpus rather than a comprehensive systematic literature review. Results may be influenced by Google Scholar indexing behavior, query phrasing, and manual classification decisions.
